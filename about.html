About Me

Hi! I’m Aarav, an Economics and Actuarial Science student at the University of Southampton with a strong interest in statistics, data, and mathematical modelling.

I’ve always enjoyed understanding why things happen rather than simply memorising formulas. Statistics fascinates me because it allows us to transform raw data into meaningful information, uncover patterns, measure uncertainty, and make better-informed decisions.

Alongside my degree, I’m developing my skills in actuarial science, statistical modelling, economics, data analysis, and programming. I’ve worked on projects involving regression, Generalised Linear Models, insurance risk, air quality, and other applications of statistics. I enjoy taking concepts that can seem intimidating at first and breaking them down into something that actually makes sense.

That idea is the foundation of this website.

I created this platform to explain statistics in a simple, structured, and practical way — from the fundamentals of data and probability to more advanced statistical methods. Instead of treating statistics as a list of equations to memorise, I want to focus on understanding what the mathematics means, why we use it, and how it applies to real-world problems.

Whether you’re learning statistics for the first time, revising for an exam, or simply curious about how data works, I hope you find something here that makes the subject clearer and more intuitive.

And most importantly, I’m still learning too. This website is part of that journey.
